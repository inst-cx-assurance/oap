# Contributing

Contributions must preserve Level 1/Level 2 separation, human authority, Outcome Auditor independence from delivery and the Outcome Director, append-only decision history in meaning, exact source traceability, and the provisional legal status. Normative changes require a source clause or approved protocol-integrity ADR plus schemas, semantic rules, fixtures, tests, and documentation. Do not include controlled-source documents, credentials, private keys, personal information, or copied normative text.
