# Authority and separation-of-duties guide

Evidence submitters, Outcome Auditors, and Outcome Directors use different assignments and action permissions. Auditor and Director must be different people; the Auditor must also be separate from evidence delivery. Assignment dates and project/outcome/boundary scope are checked at action time. Conflict declarations are mandatory for action-bearing roles in the reference semantics. Delegation cannot change role type or expand scope.

Competence and appointment fields record a declared basis. Technical validation cannot prove their truth or legal effectiveness outside the package.
