# ADR-0004: Provisional lifecycle integrity controls

- Status: Provisional; ICxA governance approval required

Expiry, withdrawal, revocation, supersession, reassessment, delegation non-expansion, conflict-declaration timing, canonicalization, namespace, and technical conformance labels are necessary exchange-integrity decisions not fully named by ICXA-10001. They are explicit draft protocol controls and are not claimed as verbatim ICxA requirements. Governance must approve or revise them before an official release.

The neutral draft rule separates human withdrawal/revocation/supersession acts from deterministic expiry and system-detected reassessment triggers. A system detection changes no human decision by itself. Delegation cannot expand authority or bypass separation of duties. These rules may be tightened after controlled-source and governance review.
